Rooted in Legacy

Grown with purpose

Daniel and David carry NAGA Farming's conscious work.

Before Naga Malt, there was Zondiwe Motshwene: a commercial farmer, a builder and a grandfather who knew that good work should outlast a single generation.
Today, brothers Daniel and David Madubanya are carrying that belief forward through Naga Farming — turning the grain they know into ingredients worth knowing.

“We are growing more than grain. We are keeping a way of doing things alive.”
