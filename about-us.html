Our Story

NAGA FARMING

Built From a Farming Legacy

Naga Malt (Pty) Ltd grew from the farming legacy of our grandfather, Zondiwe Motshwene.

What started as a piggery grew into a commercial farming operation—and eventually inspired the next generation to take things further.

Brothers Daniel and David Madubanya took that foundation into agro-processing, turning sorghum from a farm commodity into quality malt and consumer products.
